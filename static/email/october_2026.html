📉 NEW VIDEO

I also put out a new YouTube video on Python classes... and it flopped.

Yikesss.....

I'm linking it here in case you want to see it, but I won't be offended if you don't click on it.

It's a funny one how you can spend a bunch of time on something, and it doesn't quite get the traction you were hoping for.

For some context to my YouTube channel, I've produced 26 videos over the course of ~2 years, and the average amount of time I spend on each video ranges from 3 hours to 15+ hours.

You don't need to be a mathematician to work out that I've spent a lot of time on my YouTube channel, but it's hard to quantify the value returned from the channel. For example, I haven't directly made any money from it, but I know for a fact that it helped me get a job at both Meridian and now Optima.

So perhaps it has created value, it's just harder to see it directly?

Regardless, the point I'm trying to come around to is that in life, not everything you produce is going to be a hit, but even more so, neither does it need to be. I think 10cc said it best when they said "art for art's sake, money for god's sake"
