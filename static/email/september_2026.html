🧠 ONE PROBLEM A DAY

Stupid LeetCode Meme

I've started doing one LeetCode problem and one DataLemur problem every day (along with Duolingo German, because those cheeky f*cks hit me with a 1 year fee after a free sign-up).

Anyway, LeetCode is mainly for Python / general programming, while DataLemur is to practice my SQL skills.

I'll say that I've been able to solve a few of the easy array style questions, but DSA is going to be an uphill battle for me, as even easy stuff like "reverse a linked list" is still a bit of a struggle.

DataLemur on the other hand has been seeing steady improvments from me, especially when I learnt that you can chain multiple CTEs together to solve more complex problems.

It's quite an interesting little daily ritual, it likely won't scale while I have a full-time job, but it's been good to expose the large number of gaps in both my SQL, as well as DSA knowledge.
