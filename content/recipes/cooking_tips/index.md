## Overview

* Prep before you start

* Taste as you go

* Manage heat, don't turn it up because your inpatient. Understand that your pan retains residual heat.

* Dry food before browning.

* Let meat rest (*where appropriate*)

* Be patient, the more you rush food, generally the worse it will taste (*some exceptions to this, but don't take a sauce off, until it's had time to simmer*)

* Timing things up to finish cooking at the same time.

* Take care of your gear and it will take car of you (*Sharp knives make for good cooking. High quality, well maintained pans don't stick*)

* Minimization of effort (*I like to see knive magents on walls, hanging pots and pans from the roof. all pans handles on a right angle etc.*)

* Clean as you go

* **Don't overcrowd the fucking pan.**

* Stay active, stay present (*don't leave the dish to fucking burn*)

* Average chef does not need expensive gear, you can find good quality in second-hand shops, or sometimes on discount.

* Big chopping board (*it's harder to clean, but nicer to chop on*)
