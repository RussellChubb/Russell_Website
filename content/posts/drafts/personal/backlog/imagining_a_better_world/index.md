My core philosophy with this one is that currently the world is designed to prioritize the creation of wealth, when it should be to create conditions that allow for human flourishing.

How could we actually do this? - It's something that I struggle with sometimes. One of the quirks of capitalism is that it's harder to imagine a different system in place, than it is to imagine the world blowing up. That quotes probably attributable to Mark Fisher, author of capitalist realism, a book that most people won't engage with because it's terse and a little bit boring, unless you're interested in that kind of stuff.

If you've never heard of solar-punk, it's the idea that all of this technology, will allow is us to live more dignified, lives in parallel with the environment, rather than against it.

## Ideas

* Decouple "*success*" from GDP. [Social]

* Set goals for the country to achieve and then set very specific measurement criteria for evaluating success, beyond just GDP. Review progress each quarter, makes changes as the context changes, be loose, be cool. [Social]

* Move away from Americian specific technology, it creates a dependency on them, as well as introduced vulnerabilities into our IT infrastruture. [IT]

* Promote thinking about things in completely different ways, as well as being critical of the staus quo, and assumptions. [Social]

* Spend more time with friends and family. [Social]

* Spend time repairing the dividing world that media has created and create a focus on small community. [Social]

* Creating a free and open media free from insitutional power. [Media]

* Create "Ministry of Power", a fully anonymous organisation (*to prevent the inflitration and manipulation of those with wealth*) with the purpose of limiting the power of wealth. [Political]

* Worry less about paying bills through UBI. [Economic]

* Devise a method to access long term impacts of changes, we're too beholden to the 3 year political cycle, I don't reccomend extending term limits, but, there needs to be a way to access whether or not a specific policy or implementation has had the real world impact it proposed, beyond the limits of party. [Political]

* We have so much stuff, we have enough clothes for six generations of people. Perhaps it's time to create a culture of re-use, the right to repair and recyling. [Social]

* Shorter working weeks. [Economic]

* Thread people to small-business's where possible, rather than large conglomerates. [Social/Economic]

* Reduce surveillance of every day people and ban invasive telemtry. [IT]

* Deprioiritize consumer culture and rich lavish lifestyles. [Social]

* Creating a open and transpearent government that is accessible to all citizens. How much money comes in, how much gets spent, which deals are being made, by who, when etc. [Political/Economic]

* Rewarding acutal value created in society, which means paying teachers, medicial professionals, sanitation staff more. [Social/Economic]

* Move to more of a collectivist, rather than individualist world. [Social/Economic]

* Promoting values that lead to a better world, rather than one that promotes the creation of wealth. [Social]

* Live more in line with nature and the natural world around us. Rather treating the world as a resource to harness to increase wealth. [Social]

* Prioritize green energy buildouts, (*with the understanding that we still need base-loading generation of some kind to be able to cater to demand fluxuations*). [Energy]

* Allow people to life dignified lives, filled with purpose, regardless of the role they live in. [Social]

* Promoting education. [Education]

* Design cities to be more publically acessible, with more green-space, garden, public art, local ammenities etc. [Infrastructure]

* Re-deploy the influx of retried baby-boomers into teaching / guiding posistions to enable the transistion of intergenerational wisdom. [Social/Education]

* Create bi-partisian agreement on what we want to achieve in the world, then, set aside faction differences and move towards those goals in a way that rewards slow steady progress that moves beyond the 3 year political cycle. [Social/Political]

* Ban social media outright. [IT]

* Increase tax on alcohol and cigarettes. Pay people to only drink a certain amount, or not smoke? [Health]

* Taxing billionaries at 50% [Economic]

* Tax "bad-foods" takeaway, fizzy drink, not real food. Use the tax dollars to subsidize fresh fruit, vegetables, meat. [Health]
