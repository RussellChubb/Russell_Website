## 1st - 🇨🇿

* Woke up feeling not too bad for only 4 hours of sleep! - packed our bags and headed to the train station, then caught our bus to Prague.
* Walked to our Accomodation from train station, we found out it’s a bit of a boiler-room shoebox.
* Met up with Grace, Vikky, Fletch & Kaylee at about 12:00 AM
* Had some drinks then went to an Irish Pub, the MC selected me out of the crowd to sing the last song (I chose Party in the USA)
* On our way to the next club, we basically witnessed a small fight wherein a man was rendered unconscious from a punch from another bloke, he basically fell backwards and smashed his head open. We ended up calling Czech 111, (112) - The police arrived and we made our exit, not before some dude came and shook up a beer and squirted the guy with it. (Not sure why he did that)

## 2nd - 🇨🇿

* Woke up at about mid-day on account of us going to bed at 3:00AM
* Went to get a feed from the supermarket and ate it at a local park.
* Fed the pigeons and watched them turn into little Italians.
* Just watched a bunch of episodes of Outlander until about 8PM when we went to a Medieval Dinner Experience (Place was called Upavouka).
    * One hell of an experience aye.
    * I was skeptical about wether or not I’d make it out at that point, as I’d basically just started shitting myself at that point (Think it was the kebab from the previous night), but I sort of forgot about it once we got there.
    * When we crossed the threshold of the ground-level door, I could immediately hear the chanting and banging of tables downstairs. As we approached I was taken aback by the amount of people in the room, with my rough guess being about 60-70 people in a suit subterranean basement.
    * The room was dimly lit, and adorned with flame lighting, the employees were dressed in Medieval garb and were serving Ales in pot glasses and wine from Goblets.
    * We were entertained with fights, dancing from scantily clad women all while scoffing down a five course meal and drinking as much as we could (to get our moneys worth you see)
* After our meal, we went to a club called “Epic”, it wasn’t that epic, and at that point, my stomach was feeling increasingly worse.

## 3rd - 🇨🇿

* I’d probably say that this was the day that Olivia and I suffered the most on. I mentioned that we were in a bit of a boiler-room shoebox, but today was particularly bad, as we ended up spending most of our time in that room sweating.
* I only really got to bed at 4:00AM (was on the bog all night), and didn’t wake up feeling much better.
* Made a run to the supermarket to pick up more toilet-paper, along with some bread and bananas.
    * Started feeling more human late in the afternoon and went up to a beer-garden on the hill with Caylee, Fletch + Vikky and Grace.
* I could tell that the crew was pretty tired, so I didn’t even get a drink, however, we had a chat for a bit.
* We all walked down the hill and said our good-byes and then I went to McDonalds to get dinner for Olivia and I (I had a craving after only eating bread and water for like a day)
* All in all, we saw fuck all of Prague, and thus we’ll probably have to come back another time to do it again properly! - Oh well.
