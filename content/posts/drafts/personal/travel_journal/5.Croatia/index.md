## 26th - 🇭🇷

On the day of the 26th, we checked out of our accomodation, and headed to a laundromat to dry our clothes, which were still wet from the load we did last night. After finally finding a laundromat, Olivia sat with our clothes, and I headed to a supermarket to purchase some food, which we ended up eating in the laundromat.

After that, we packed up our clothes and headed to the docks to wait for our ferry, which we were like 7 hours early for, so to pass the time, we set-up at a restraunt, ordered some drinks, I ate a wonderful seafood pasta, and played cards while trying to hide from the sun in some shade.

While waiting for the ferry, Olivia had a bout of food poisioning or something these lines, and almost had an accident while we were waiting for our Ferry.

Regardless, the 7 hours passed, and we were at the front of the line to get onto the ferry. However, one snag we (*and some other girls we met*) ran into, is that all we had was a boarding pass, which didn't get us onto the ferry, instead, the gentlemen at the gate told us that in order to get our "*tickets*", we'd need to catch a bus to a completly different place, get our tickets, and then came back, and as we were walking away I could almost hear him say "*stupid tourists*" under his breath. So, red-faced we went to the bus-stop, and started chatting to some australian chicks who ran into the same situation as us. So, on the bus chatting away about how we might not make it, and how the ferry might leave without us etc etc, but in the end, we arrived back in plenty of time to get on the ferry.

Onboard the ferry was pretty good, we chucked down our bags, grabbed a meal, and then came back to the lounge. For reference, we paid slighty extra for a lounge, but didn't pay enough for a room, however, my mum told us that you can recline on the lounges quite well, and catch some z's that way.

Little did I know this wasn't quite correct, the lounges would have been perfect for a 3 hour trip, but to actually sleep there, it had some issues:

1) The AC was cranked super low, however, we'd barely packed any warm clothes because it's summer time
2) We couldn't recline across the seats, because they were designed with anti-homeless person technology to make sleeping impossible.

Regardless, we managed to eventually make it to sleep.

On the 27th, we arrived in Split, Croatia, tired and weary after a restless night and peaved that we didn't shell out for cabins. We were both hungry, so we went to a restraunt and ordered some burgers and fries at about 9:00AM, which I thought was quite novel. We then caught our bus to Dubrovnik, and enjoyed looking out the window at the Croatian country-side.

We arrived at the port, and then caught a taxi to our accomodation, wherein we met up with Hayden, Leila, Kayleigh, Sam, Ben, Keegan, Holly and Sophie. After some hugs and introductions, Hayden, Olivia and I decided to go to the beach, as we were all sweaty, smelly and tired from our travels.

I'll say one thing right now, Croatian beaches are some of the best in the world. They're mostly rocky, however, the water is clear blue, and the cliff-sides are spectacular.

The other thing about Croatia is that they love cliff-jumping, kids as old as nine jumping 20 metres into the air and then the water. Not to be out-done by children, Hayden and I decide to climb up to the highest point (*admittedly, not really that high*), and then jump off. Now, all went well with my jump, but unfortunately, Hayden's shoulder popped out of it's socket, so I had to paddle him to the shore to help him out of the water. Luckily this all ended well, as his should naturally popped back in, but it was a tad scary for a bit!

After the swim, and feeling much more refreshed, we went back to the accomodation, hit the corner store, picked up some drinks and started getting ready for a big night out. We started drinking in the largest of our three rooms, and eventually we messaged someone about acquiring some Coke and Ketamine, which eventually materalized into the corners of my nasal cavities.

## 28th - 🇭🇷

After a heavy night last night, we all woke-up around mid-day and grabbed a bite to eat, before making our way to the beach to wash off last nights sins.

I mentioned earlier that Croatians are a bit weird when it comes to nudity, as such, Hayden and I went to a bar on the beach to get a beer, and the waiter basically said "*you're at a restraunt, put a shirt on*", now we didn't make a fuss, because why would we, but I'm kind of confused at why he took issue with us not wearing shirts, despite having a bar on the beach? I just couldn't wrap my australasian brain around it.

Other than that, the beach was awesome, although Ben and Sam both stepped on sea urchins (*in Croatia, they're everywhere on the shoreline*). After the beach, we started getting ready to go out again, and I'll read out one of my notes that I wrote down while I was fairly sauced "*I played frog ambience from my phone for 3 hours and no-one noticed*".

Nice.

## 29th - 🇭🇷

On the 29th, we spent the daylight hours on a guided kayak tour around Dubrovnik, which was super awesome. We learnt about the cursed island, and got taken to some cool spots to swim.

After that, we went for a walk around Dubrovnik, before going home to get changed. For dinner we had some burgers that we were all really craving due to us building up a strong hunger from all the walking and kayaking.

We ended up watching the Brazil vs Japan world cup match, but after a couple of nights of back to back drinking, the vibes were pretty mellow. After the game finished, we decided to save our strength, and walked back to the accomodation and called it a night.

When we arrived in Hvar, we were amazed at how many luxury yachts were sitting in the harbour, it was instanity. After picking up our collective jaws, we trekked up a hill to our next accomodation. Once we were all unpacked, we decided to just get a cheap feed for dinner, which resulted in Olivia and I sharing some beers and chips + dip between us while sitting on a dock watching storm clouds roll in.

It was only after the first real crash of thunder that we decided to make our way back to the accomodation, thus finishing off the day.

## 2nd - 🇭🇷

The next day, we all wake with heavy heads, however, one our our friends wakes with a head and heart heavier than most as Keegan starts freaking out saying "*fuck I fell in the ocean last night*".

For some context, unbeknowst to us, our good mate Keegan got seperated from the pack late last night, so while he was walking home alone, he fell into the Hvar habour, wherein he told us the locals all laughed at him as he struggled to get out of the water (*there isn't any ladders or anything in the harbour, and it's full of boats*).

To make matters worse however, Keegan tells us that he fell into the water not just with his phone, but also his passport. To end the story on a more positive note, Keegan did end up getting both his phone, and passport back alright (*albeit a little bit thicker due to the salt water*).

Now, due to my state last night, I woke up with one of the worst hangovers of my life, however, the day before we had rented a boat and a skipper to take us to some cool beaches, which is probably the last thing anyone hungover actually wants. However, I dragged myself to the boat, and the skipper roared the engine and before I knew it, we were zooming out of the harbour.

The cold wind on my face I'll admit was a good step 1 for curing my hangover, but, it was only when I first dove into the water that step 2 of removing my hangover started working wonders. The third and final step of removing my hangover was a cured meat and cheese sandwhich that lined my stomach, that I was able to declare to the world that I was bright eyed, bushy tailed, and now hangover free.

The skipper of the boat took us to a few different spots around the coast, however, he saved the best for last, as we ended up at this cute little bar tucked into the coastline. It was at this spot that one of my funnier memories comes from this trip comes from, as Ben's cheese from the ingredients he used to make his sandwhich, ended up slipping out of the packet, and ended up coagulating in the bottom of his bag.

Much less funny was that I manageed to get super sunburnt, despite applying sunblock 4 times liberally throughout the day.

Anyway, we all went to bed early as we were all super tired, and still slightly hungover, plus we had a travel day the next day.

## 3rd - 🇭🇷

On the third, we all checked out of the accomodation, wherein we walked to the dock, and said goodbye to Leila, Sam and Ben, before taking off on different paths.

For Olivia, Keegan, Leila, Kayleigh and I, we had one more night in Dubrovnik before our respective flights back home. As such, we got off from our ferries, battled to find a taxi, and then almost had a bit of a meltdown trying to get into our next accomodation.

For the rest of the day, we decided to go to a bar and watched Australia get knocked out of the world cup on penalities, before all agreeing that we were quite tired, and headed back to the accomodation for a 11:00 PM bedtime.
