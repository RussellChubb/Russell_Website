## 4th - 🇩🇪

On the day of the 4th, I awoke feeling much better (*albeit it with a massive pimple on my lip lol.*)

Olivia and I said "*goodbye*" to our terrible accomodation and then walked to the bus-station where we caught a 5 hour bus to Munich.

After arriving in Munich, and then catching a train out to Pasing, we arrvied at our accomodation (*I picked a slightly nicer one this time to make up for how shit our previous one was lol*), and “*monged*” out for the rest of the day before hitting the supermarket to catch a light feed, before calling it a day.

## 5th - 🇩🇪

On the 5th, we were determined to start bouncing back from our rough experience in Prague.

We started the day by getting some cash out such that we could go to the local fruit and vegetable market and buy some berries and a pretzel! We also went on a mad adventure to wash our clothes, (*which at this point were in dire need of it*)

After that, we caught a train back into Munich and then picked up a supermarket lunch for later on! When we arrived back in central Munich, we took a tram to the Eisbachwelle (*The famous river surfing spot in Munich*). It was for sure a highlight for Olivia and I to see the girls (*and guys*) tearing it up on that river-wave. Fun fact, it's also the worlds longest man-made lazy river!

Afterwards, to beat the heat, Olivia and I picked up some Matcha Milkshakes, before walking around central Munich, wherein Olivia was star-struck by the Siemans Building (*She uses a lot of Siemans machines for her job*).

After that, we had a look around a few stores before catching a train back to Pasing, and calling it a day.

## 6th - 🇩🇪

On the 6th, we woke to a slight more overcast day and aftre showering, we ended up at a mall in Pasing. I'm not sure how I always find myself at malls, considering how much I dislike them.

After my mall experience, we picked up these delicious roast pork sandwhiches, which we then had for dinner.

The last of my notes for this day is that the shower in our room was clogged, and so Olivia didn't notice this, but she basically flooded the entire room, which in retrospect was quite funny, although we were slightly worried at the time.

After getting in plenty of steps, we decided we needed a cold drink somewhere, and were lucky to come across a Sommerwerft, which is a Frankfurt Summer festival with entertainment, food drink and music. Olivia and I had a very nice couple of hours listening to songs, drinking some beers, and playing some cards. I'll quickly note here how many bees there are in Germany, and Europe generally. They don't really bother me, but I felt as though every 5 minutes Olivia was losing her mind slapping away a bee or wasp.

As the day started to wind down, we went out for dinner at a place called Kiwi's, which had super delicious food and a very kind waitress, who found it quite entertaining about how two New Zealander came to a place called Kiwis (*For reference the restraunt had nothing to do with New Zealand, and everything to do with the bird*)

This next bit still pains me to think and write about, but on our way back to our accomodation, because it was only five minutes by train, we decided to be a bit cheeky and not pay for our train fare... **Big Mistake**.

The inspector stepped on, questioned us, and because I replied with "*Ich habe vergessen, mein ticket zu kaufen*" (*I forgot to buy my ticket*), instead of something like "*Ich wusste nicht, dass ich ein Ticket brauche*" (*I didn't know we needed a ticket*) we got fined $120 NZD. This was a massive kick in the pants! - (*what makes it even worse was that we were at our stop!*).

By the way, I love the way that German sentences are structured.

> Ich habe vergessen, mein ticket zu kaufen
> I have forgotten, my ticket to buy
