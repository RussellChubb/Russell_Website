## 4th - 🇬🇷

On the 4th we awoke at 8:00AM, said our goodbyes to Leila, Kayleigh and Keegan, and then took an Uber out to the airport. I remember being upset at Olivia for being a bit of a bitch, which caused a bit of a spat outside the airport. However, I think we got past it quite quickly, and ended up chilling at the airport for most of the day, with both of us taking cat-naps at different points throughout the day.

One of the funny things from the airport in Dubrovnik that continued later on was how some people seem to turn up to airports and act like they've never travelled before. We were in front of a dude and his family, and he hadn't paid for any baggage, despite having like 5 massive bags. My brother in christ, how the fuck did you order a ticket and not work out that you need to add on bags? Anyway, I'm only mad about this, because despite being very early in line, we ended up getting checked in last.

The actual flight to Athens went off without a hitch, and we maanged to catch a train from the airport to Yellow Square Hostel (Athens), arriving about 9:30PM, which gave us just enough time to brush our teeth, and get into bed. Olivia didn't want to eat any dinner this night, as it was around this point that she was starting to come down with a bit of a cold.

## 5th - 🇬🇷

After a night of tossing and turning, I awoke on the 5th, packed our bags and took a train out to the Athens port, wherein Olivia scared me half to death after we got into line and she said "*I’m not sure this is the right ferry*". However, we asked around, and as it turned out, it was the right ferry.

Onboard the ferry, Olivia seemed to be deteroirating by the minute, however, she's got this elite skill where if she’s not doing well in some capacity (*sick, hungover etc etc*), she’ll just got into Hibernation mode. I wish I had this skill.

When we arrived in Santorini It was crazy getting off the boat at first, the door opened, and I thought we were storming the beach at normandy, looking straight up at a vertical cliff-face, with about 4 buildings, I remember wondering "*where the fuck am I*". After overcoming my shell-shock, I walked up to a man who looked like he knew what he was doing asking him about where I can find a taxi to our accomodation, he pointed us to a bloke, and after an exchange of funds, and 15 minutes of waiting, we started the journey up the side of one of the most vertical cliff-faces I've ever seen.

After a small joruney by van, we arrived at Caveland Hostel at around 4:30 PM. I'll speak a little bit about Caveland, which ended up being my favourite hostel that I've stayed in. If you're wondering about the name, the place is literally built into caves, and the staff / owners are super cool.

Anyway, back to the story, Olivia and I unpack our stuff and then go out to get a meal. Around this point, Olivia was genuinely weak sauce. Bro was fading, saying she was having a out of body experience, and that kind of chat. After dinner (*which was really nice, I had a chicken and vegetable meal with a Rossini, and Olivia had a slow cooked beef dish with some kind of carb thing.*), we walked back to our accomodation, wherein we met our flatmates, one guy was a nice australian bloke called (*name*), and another bloke from New Zealand, who funnily enough, was filling in on Saxophone for the Butlers when Olivia and I went to their gig. Anyway, those two went out, and Olivia and I stayed in (*because of her condition*).

## 6th - 🇬🇷

<!-- What was the curly haired dudes name? -->

The next day I woke up and went for a quick swim at the Hostel Pools, wherein I met another kiwi bloke called (*name*). Olivia and I then dropped our clothes off at a laundromat, before we walked into Fira (*town nearest to where we were staying*) to look at the shops.

We then proceeded to have a walk around Fira, picked up some gyros from Lucki's (*a place that a member of staff at the Hostel reccomended*), which were super yummy! We then walked back to our accomodation, picking up our laundry on the way back, before getting changed into our togs, and going for a bit of a workout session, before having a swim to cool off.

As the sun started dropping low, we started getting ready to go out, before playing a few drinking games, and then heading to a club called Highlander, which is a super fun spot that you can dance on a stage and on tables.

## 7th - 🇬🇷

The 7th was one of the highlight days for me, as Olivia and I went on a "*sunset cruise*" with some of the Caveland crew, which was a super fun time.

During the cruise, we were taken around the various coves and bays around Santorini, with our skipper giving us information about each of the spots, as well as the captain anchoring us down so we could go swim in differet areas. There was also unlimited drinks, a tasty dinner, good people, and immaculate vibes. Honestly, what more could you ask for?

I also enjoyed how we did "*snorklies*", which is basically where you tip a drink down the snorkle, and you've got to drink it all before you drown. Super fun, and also a little salty.

After the cruise, we went back to Caveland and pre-drank with everyone, before running it back to Highlander. Olivia and I left a little earlier this time, stopped at Maccas for a feed (*Olivia couldn't find McDonalds when it was right behind her*), and then walked home.

## 8th - 🇬🇷

After the big night, I woke up feeling absolutely shattered (*little did I know this was going to be more than just a hangover*).

I spent most of the day chilling by the pool, Olivia was in bed most of the day as well.

Later on in the day, we started feeling peckish, so we walked into Fira for more gyros. As the energy levels were pretty low, we called it a day and went to bed at 9:00PM.

## 9th - 🇬🇷

On the 9th I woke up feeling sick (*although thankfully a bit better than the day before*), for our travel-day to Paros Island. A taxi took us down the cliff-face, dropping us off at that same port I was gawking at just a few days prior.

After arriving in Paros, we hired a quad bike and drove to the other side of the island to get to our hotel. I'll note that hiring the quad bike was a little expensive, but was well worth the money. Also the accomodation in Paros we were staying in was right by the beach, and the day-beds were just what the doctor ordered.

To finish off the day, we spent some time chilling on the sunbeds, and I went for a swim before we headed out for dinner. It aroudn dinner-time on this day that I realised how sick I was, as I realised I'd lost my sense of smell and taste, which was less than ideal.

## 10th - 🇬🇷

I was still sick on the 10th, and still couldn't taste or smell anything. To make matters worse, I also clogged the toilet. A great start to the day.

Around mid-day, we took the quad out for a bit of a tiki tour around the island and went to a place called Faragas. Now I probably wouldn't go back to Faragas, as it was super expensive and, to be honest, kind of overrated. After that, we headed back to our accomodation and spent the rest of the day chilling on the day-beds.

## 11th - 🇬🇷

The 11th was a much more relaxed day. We tanned on the sunbeds for most of it, with the occasional break to go for a swim.

Later in the day, we ventured back into Parikia (*Paros Central*) to go take a look around and spotted some kind of beach basketball tournament going on. I didn't even know beach basketball was a thing, but there you go.

We went out for dinner in Parikia, and afterwards I had a go at skimming rocks. On the drive back to our accomodation in the dark, I almost crashed the ATV. Hehe.

## 12th - 🇬🇷

The 12th was another travel day, this time catching the ferry from Paros back to Athens. I did a bit of coding on the boat and the time flew by.

Once we got into Athens, we took the train back to Yellow Square and then went for a walk around Athens, with the idea to end up at the Acropolis.

After meandering through Athens, we arrived at the Acropolis, however, we were told that we'd arrived a bit too late, so we couldn't go in. However, we had a fun time walking around the outskirts of the Acropolis and taking in the history.

After that we started feeling a bit peckish, so we went out for a meal, (*wherein Olivia's eyes were bigger than her belly*). We also made a friend in the form of a cat with the world's biggest eyes, who was more than happy to trade cuddles for our left-overs.

## 13th - 🇬🇷

We woke up on the 13th, checked out of Yellow Square, and headed to the train station. This train was so funny, as it kind of physced us 3 times, as a train would come, then turn off, leave, another would do the same thing and the same thing, until a train finally arrvied to take us to the airport (*They really didn't like Olivia, apparently*)

We were told in Santorini that you need to make sure to arrive early to Athens airport, as it's super busy. So, you can imagine that after getting fluffed around by the trains and losing 30 minutes, we were already stressed by the time we got to Athens Airport. Now, our friends in Santorini were right, Athens Airport was crazy busy, with so many hurdles to jump through and lines to wait in. Olivia and I almost missed our flight, but thankfully a lady pulled us out of the lines, and dragged us to our gate. On the bright side, we saw Giannis Antetokounmpo (*top 10 basketball player in the world*) at the airport.

With that, we ended our Greek Adventure, breathed in the cold Scottish air, caught a tram to Kayleighs, and hit the hay (*not before hitting that shit outside of the flat*).
