Our first travel day (*of many more to come*), we took a few different lines to get out to Luton (*validate this*) Airport, wherein we hopped on old faithful (*a Ryanair flight*), before getting dropped off in Barcelona Airport, met up with Mum and Dad, and then took a Taxi into the city to our shared AirBNB (*thanks for having us at the accomodation mum and dad <3 )

Once we got to the AirBNB, we unpacked and went out for a afternoon cervsa (*beer*) and a bite to eat. Now, the first place we went to was a little tapa's place on the corner, and after trying the waiters patience with my terrible Spanish, we were presented with a meal of pata's brava's, pork tacos and beer.

I want to highlight how high this meal set my expectations for food in Europe, because the very first place we went to, had the most amazing food. So good in fact that we went back maybe like two more times, and the meals we had still remain one of the highlights of our trip. (*I feel like this is funny because it’s not like the place was known for its food or anything*).

## 27th - 🇪🇸

My father and I had one objective on this day, get our suits fitted! So, after starting the day with a light breakfast, we took a Taxi out with Olivia and Mum. Once we got to the store, we were greeted by a lady who spoke no English at all, however, she still got us fitted out.

However, we were unable to finalize some of the details of the arrangement due to the language barrier. She then called in a man who I assume was her son, to try and help us out. Funnily enough he still didn't speak any English, so this called for the big guns (*Google Translate*), after which we were able to provide him the deposit and we were on our way.

We then stopped for a quick coffee (*and Juice for Olivia*), before mum and dad took a taxi back to the accomodation, and Olivia and I decided to go for a walk to explore the city by foot, (*might I add quite a good city to explore by foot*). We then proceeded to walk to a place for a feed (*I mainly decided to stop at this particular place because I saw a group of swaggy young people outside*). Now, one of my favourite foods from my 2023 visit to Spain was Padron peppers, which are these specific peppers that are fried in oil with salt and pepper. Anyway, Olvia isn't exactly the most adventerous when it comes to food, but I'm sure she's glad that she tried the Padron peppers, as she ended up realling enjoying them! After letting our food and mid-day beer digest, we then walked over to La Sagrada Famila.

Now, when I visited La Sagrada in 2023, you didn't need to book in advance to go inside, however, when we arrived, and I rolled up to the counter expecting to go straight through, I was sorely mistaken when they turned me away after telling me I needed a booking. So instead, we decided to walk around all four facades, while I read out La Sagrada trivia to Olivia that I was ripping from Wikipedia.

After that, we purchased out patch and fridge magnet for Barcelona, and then Olvia went to get her nails done, and I to get a haircut. Now, I haven't written this here yet, but if you didn't know, the main reason we went to Barcelona first was because my brother Rian was getting married! As such, my mum convinced me that mullets aren't welcome at the wedding, and so I made the critical mistake of getting mine cut off during my haircut.

Then, (*with cooler ears*), we walked to the main drag to take a look at the shop's and the Gaudi Building (*which is pretty dope might I add*). Olivia ended up buying some cheap shoes for the wedding, and I purchased some running shoes!

## 28th - 🇪🇸

We awoke to another blue sky, 30 degree day in Barcelona, however, before the mid-day heat set-in, we decided to go for a run to see the city by foot. Now, this is such a noble idea, but to execute this successfully, you actually have to leave your house before 6:00AM, as by the time we were leaving, the sun was well and truly up, and we absolutely baked during this run, sweat was absoutely pouring out of me every which way. In the end we still put up a valiant ~ 10 KM effort from our accomodation to the beach, however it definently took it out of us.

After we showered and ate a light brekkie, we then went shopping. Now for some reference, I don't really like shopping, it's not really in my DNA. So much of the day I was likely sulking about. However, my mood drastically changed as we started doing some drinking, which I want to note that if there's something that you really don't want to do, just start drinking. (*delete this*)

## 30th - 🇪🇸

The day of Rian and Maria's wedding I awoke with a code-red hangover, sweating, shaking headaches, and my stomach was doing backflips (*Not good*). So I proceeded to down some food, put my suit on, and make our way to the bus which was set to take us out to the location of the wedding. However, the first stop on the bus was to the actual church for the ceremony, wherein I was the ring bearer (*in lieue of any small children attending the wedding*). As part of these responsibilites, I didn't have to just walk up with the rings, I also had to read a passage from the bible to the congregation, which, in my condition, combined with regular nerves, prooved to be an interesting challenge. However, I got it done, and after some beautiful photos, we where shuttled back onto the bus for our final destination for the evening.

I don't remember much about the second, longer bus-trip, other than looking down trying not to simultaneoulsy shit myself and throw up. Regardless, we made it to the venue, where I proceeded to b-line it to the bathrooms. To change the tone from my horrible state, I want to make mention of how beautiful this venue was called (*find out name*) and was out in the Barcelona country-side, and is still to this day, one of the most beautiful venue's I've ever seen.

People we starting to drink at this point, but I could barely even stand to look at one. Some of the food being served wasn't helping either, as it was airing towards the experimental side of the culinary spectrum. Afterwards we were moved to the dining area, wherein I was greeted with our table-mates.

Now, one thing I haven't told you about is how each table was assigned a mission, for example, one table needed to get everyone doing a conga line, the other had to make the venue do a mexican wave. However, for our particular table, I was the "*captain*", however, I was struggling to work out what Maria had written down as our task (*due to her linked penmanship*), as such, I was showing everyone the writing, trying to decode her writing. It was only when I realised that Maria had told me that me that "*Olivia and you are on the shit table*" was I finally able to decode the message which spelled out "*Russell, we've put you and Olivia at the boring table, your mission is to be the life of the party*", anyway, if it wasn't for Maria's hand-writing, they'd both be in some interesting conversations.

Anyway, I mentioned that some of the cuisine was particularly, "*adventerous*", which was a trend that continued up until the main meal, which was this loaf of slow cooked duck meat. Now you might be wondering why I'm focusing on this blob of meat? Well, I credit this blob of meat for providing the lining to my stomach to start my second-wind, which for reference, prior to this, I wasn't doing well.

Now I want to make mention that my brother is a quirky guy, and I feel like this showed through in some of the activities / food / celebrations during the wedding. For some examples of this, there were some sumo suits that people (*and later me*) hopped into and jumped around, which was great fun, until you almost over-heated. There was also a few other things, hotdogs being served at 11:30, pigeon masks, a giant sword to cut the wedding cake etc. All in all though, I loved the combination of traditional cathloic and modern excentricity.

## 31st - 🇪🇸

Our final full day in Spain! Olivia and I both woke-up feeling pretty well, considering we still had quite a few drinks at the wedding.

This was the day that we met-up with Hayden, who had just arrived with Josh, and had straight from the plane to his hostel, to then meet us in Plaça De Catalunya for a few drinks. I remember looking at Hayden's eyes, and the thought still causes my eyes to hurt, as I knew the feeling all to well of travelling all the way from New Zealand to Europe.

Anyway, after saying goodbye to Hayden and Josh, we headed back to the accomodation, packed up our stuff slightly, and then started planning our Italy Leg of our trip.
