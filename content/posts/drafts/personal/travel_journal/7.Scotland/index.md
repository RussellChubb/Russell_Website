## 14th - 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁳󠁣󠁴󠁿

We awoke on the 14th in an almost alien climate, after a month and a half in European summer, the cool breeze on my face was an almnost unnerving sensation.

After a light feed, Olivia and I spent the day op-shopping, where I picked up a sick cable-knit jumper that I still enjoy wearing as I write this in Autum. In particular, the store on Cockburn street was very good.

We also visited Edinburgh Castle and walked along the Royal Mile wherein we spotted a very loud, red-haired, street-performer with fire.

Afterwards, we met up with Kayleigh and Leila, and had a beer Raeburn Pub to close out the day.

## 15th - 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁳󠁣󠁴󠁿

<!-- TODO -->

Now the big reason Olivia and I travelled back across Europe to the United Kingdom was so that we could see Olivia's parents + Kayleigh and Jeanie, and join them their North Coast 500 Road Trip. FYI: The North Coast 500 (NC500) is a 516-mile (830 km) scenic coastal road trip route in the North Highlands of Scotland

As such, the 15th marks the start of the road-trip, wherein our first stop was a place called Cupar, so Tim could investigate the Horsbrugh family tree. While Tim was finding good resources at the Cupar Library, I was finding good resources in the form of a tasty steak and haggis pie.

The next stop was St Andrews, where we had a good walk around the town, the old course (*as a golf enthusiast, it was pretty cool*) and investigated some of the castle ruins by the beach.

Dinner was at a Wetherspoons (*For any Kiwi's Wetherspoons is like a chain restraunt*), where I spilled a beer on the table, but Olivia got the blame. We called it an early night at the place we were staying — I still need to find its name.

## 16th - 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁳󠁣󠁴󠁿

Fyvie Castle was so sick. The site is said to be haunted by the Green Lady, and the gardens were beautiful. We took a tour and had some kind of fish soup for lunch, then headed to a whisky distillery for another tour and a tasting. We stayed in Tornagrain that night.

## 17th - 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁳󠁣󠁴󠁿

We visited Culloden Battlefield, where I tripped up the stairs, and then went to Clava Cairns. In Inverness, we picked up meal deals and ate them beside the river.

We also stopped at Loch Ness and visited the castle there, which felt like a waste of money. Russell was sick, so we went to bed early.

## 18th - 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁳󠁣󠁴󠁿

Russell was still sick, though maybe starting to feel a bit better. Along the way, we saw offshore wind turbines and stopped in Golspie, where we got to move around on the funny machines. We were there for about five minutes.

In Helmsdale, Olivia and Kayleigh were cold, and Olivia ate a King Rib. We also visited a museum. There was a cliff area too — I think it might have been Wagalo Steps? We carried on along the North Coast 500, which is the route we’re doing, and ended up at a pub in John O’Groats.

One important discovery: Scotland showers have Robocop inside them.

## 19th - 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁳󠁣󠁴󠁿

We were properly getting into the Scottish Highlands, and Russell was feeling less sick. I bought a beanie at Choraidh Croft Tearoom, and we visited the Scoo? Caves. The scenery along the roadside was lovely, and we stayed somewhere that was low-key a castle or manor.

## 20th - 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁳󠁣󠁴󠁿

We spent the day on the Isle of Skye, feeding Highland cows and visiting the Harry Potter bridge. Russell was feeling a lot less sick by then.

## 21st - 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁳󠁣󠁴󠁿

We had a run around Oban and saw the budget Colosseum. Glen Coe was so amazing. Later, we had BYO and pho, and spent some time talking politics. We stayed in Stirling.

## 22nd - 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁳󠁣󠁴󠁿

We visited the William Wallace Monument, the grounds of the Battle of Bannockburn, and Stirling Castle.

## 23rd - 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁳󠁣󠁴󠁿

I went for a 7 km run and managed to navigate back to Kayleigh’s without a map. My red shoes were gone, so I couldn’t get them. We had to check five stores for lemongrass paste.

After doing some washing, we had a lazy arvo and watched the Commonwealth Games.

## 24th - 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁳󠁣󠁴󠁿

I went for a run along the canals while Olivia went shopping. In the evening, we had dinner at the Neighbourhood and met Kayleigh’s workmates.

After dinner, we went bowling, played pool and ping pong, and checked out the arcade while having a few drinks. Olivia may have cut her hand on a bowling ball?

## 25th - 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁳󠁣󠁴󠁿

We were slightly hungover, but still managed to get out and explore. We visited the Tweed River — like the material — as well as Horsbrugh Castle and Nether Horsbrugh Castle. We also went to Horsbrugh Street and Horsbrugh Terrace.

The Kelpies were impressive, though they kind of smell bad, lol. We stopped by the Falkirk Wheel too, then had homemade burgers for dinner. It was an early bedtime ahead of our 5:45 flight.
