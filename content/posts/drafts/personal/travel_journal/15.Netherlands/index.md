## 16th - 🇳🇱

On the 16th, we awoke early and made our to the bus station to catch our bus to Amsterdam! This was such a pleasent and smooth bus ride, I remember falling asleep and then waking up in Amsterdam, wherein the whole bus managed to execute a super smooth unload of people and bags, and then left.

I don't really talk about it much, but one of the more stressful parts of travelling (*atleast for me*), was getting to the bus-stations / airports, and then actually loading stuff onto the buses, as there's usually a confluence of things going on:

* people pushing to put stuff on a bus
* very minimal space to operate in
* very new very different environment
* people that speak almost no english

Either way, this is why I made a note of why I appreciated a smooth bus trip and smooth offboarding.

Olivia and I then proceeded to try and catch a train direct to our accomodation, however, we were a minute late. No bother... Sike! Bother! We tried to catch the next train, however, conducters started shutting down almost all platforms, as some kind of technical issue was stopping trains on tracks (*just our luck lol*)

As such, we decided to walk to the next station with all of our bags, in hopes that by the time we reached the next station, the issue would be resolved, and we wouldn't have to fight hoardes of people to try and get on a train.

Other than Olivia's heavy ass backpack on my back, the actually walk was super pleasent, I enjoyed seeing the canals, the super tall girls and guys, and the classic Amsterdam architecture on the way to the other station.

Anyway, we eventually made it to our accomodation, which was a little out of the way, but nothing too crazy. The name of the place was ... (*find this out*) place had a lot of personality: we were staying in a warehouse full of caravans, with each “*room*” effectively being a caravan, beach hut, or sauna.

After settling in, we headed back into Amsterdam Central, and walked around the main drag, until we found an asian restraunt to have dinner in. While we were playing some cards after we had finished our dinner, the waitress liked that we were playing card games so much that she gave us a free bottle of soju! This was super sweet of her, however, if you've ever had unflavoured soju, you know that it can be a bit of a challenge to drink.

To walk off our dinner, we ventured into the red light district as it was beginning to get dark. This was quite fascinating (*and a little surreal*) taking a look at all the girls lined up in the windows, as I didn't think I'd seen so many fake boobs in my life.

For desert, we went to a coffee shop and shared a joint, which hit us pretty hard, and we ended up wandering around Amsterdam in a haze before heading home.

Once we arrived home, we put on a movie, and Olivia then proceeded to eat all of our snacks before we called it a night and went to bed.

## 18th - 🇳🇱

On the 18th, I had two recruiter calls at 1:30PM and 6:00PM, which kind of threw a spanner in the day.

I started the day with a trip to the Asian grocery store that we’d been visiting every day to buy savory crepes and bananas for lunch.

After the first recruiter call, Olivia and I went for a run and ended up covering a route that took us past the:

* Low Quiet Square nunnery
* Anne Frank House
* Central Square
* And a park in Amsterdam (*that I still can’t remember the name of.*)

Later, we caught up with Bram, a friend from New Zealand, and went to four different pubs together.

We were trying to make it home on the metro, but all of the trains had stopped without us realising, and we nearly got locked inside the Amsterdam metro station. Thankfully, a guy let us out before we were stuck there... Close call though!
