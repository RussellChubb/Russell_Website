## 12 - 🇧🇪

On the morning of the 12th, we awoke (*still in Luxembourg at this point*) at 7:00AM with dreams of going to the gym… and then snoozed our alarm. We eventually got up at 8:00AM and headed to the laundromat to do our washing. One thing we learned quickly was that nothing in Luxembourg really opens before noon, so after getting lucky with a 24-hour laundromat, we dropped off our clothes and walked over to a cool café that served matcha and had a Shibu Inu sleeping inside.

While walking, we also saw a woman walking six Scottish Terriers, which we were convinced was the name of the breed and still weren’t entirely sure.

When then walked back to the Laundromat, where a a guy handed me cash to do his washing (*because it was card-only*), and it wasn’t until after I’d paid for it that I realised he was short on cash... **Damn**

After checking out of our accommodation, we went for a walk around the outskirts of Luxembourg City and stumbled across a park full of newly planted trees that we mused looked like they were struggling in the heat. While walking back into town, we ran into an environmental group discussing the exact issue, and it made us realise the park really needed more shade while the trees were still young.

We walked back to Danish’s to pick up the bags we’d left there (*thanks Danish*) before catching our bus to Belgium.

When we arrived at the Belgium Bus Station, we managed to get a train out to the right spot, but this was were we got quite confused, as the location was basically smack bang in the middle of a Univeristy, School, Food Court, Housing complex, and the route that Google wanted us to go didn't take into account mult-layers, and so we ended up walking through a university campus, a construction site, and even a hospital before finally arriving at our accomodation.

## 13th - 🇧🇪

We woke up late and spent the morning taking it easy, before nipping down to the Hotel-Gym for a Hyrox session, which was going great, until the treadmill shat the bed.

We then went out, grabbed some lunch and then spent the rest of the day lounging around, with Olivia watching Netflix while Russell working on his now infamous "*2026 Data Engineering Roadmap*" article.

We finished the day with a beautiful walk, to get some fresh air in us.

## 14th - 🇧🇪

We woke up a bit earlier and hit the gym for another grueling Hyrox session, before chowing down on some of last-nights leftovers.

Once our food had digested, we caught a train into the city centre and then walked for an hour and a half to Mini-Europe, a theme park filled with miniature replicas of famous European landmarks. My core memory from this little experience was that, while it was cool, it was so so so hot. You can kind of see in some of the photos, but there isn't really any shade at the venue, and I'm so glad they had misters, or else I think I might have perished.

After we finished up at little Europe, we tried to catch a train back to the city centre, however, we were kicked off the train as police, ambulances, and fire trucks rolled in and shut down the entire block, leaving us to make the long walk back to the city centre instead.

Once we arrived back in the city centre, I was tired, hot, hungry and overwhelmed. However, we still stopped in some cool shops for a browse, before hitting my highlight of the day, which was a restraunt called La Cantina Cubana, where the food was excellent, the drinks were cheap, and the vibes were great.

After dinner, Olivia had a bit of a sweet-tooth, so we picked up some Belgian waffles, and then headed into the town centre to catch light show (*find the name of this*) at 10:00PM

## 15th - 🇧🇪

On the 15th, we woke up late and had a relaxed morning before heading into town around 1:00PM.

We ended up walking through pretty much every vintage store in Brussels, and there were tonnes of cool thrift shops to explore. We also checked out the laid flower display (*this is the same as last night, but it was cool to see during the day*), which was really nice to look at.

At one of the thrift stores (*Find the name of it*), I bought a cheese-cutter hat that ended up being slightly too small for my head, and ended up cutting off circulation to his head, (*but I was committed to the look, so I didn't remove it*).

We then took an outdoor elevator to “*The View*” in Brussels, and after wandering around a bit, we followed our noses to a market with live rock and roll and ribs, so of course we stayed for a while, ordered some drinks, and soaked up the atmosphere.

Later, we had a few drinks at an Irish pub where we both failed spectacularly at splitting the G twice, which was as funny as it was confusing. We also headed back to La Cantina Cubana for for some €5 Aperol Spritzes and Mojitos. I've got it written down here that Olivia wanted me to make a note that she beat me four or five times in a row at Uno... (*Righto big shot*).

While travelling Europe, Olivia became completely obsessed with the “*squeeze your own orange juice*” machines they have in European supermarkets. There is no conclusion to this point other than there was no chance of her catching scurvy while travelling.
