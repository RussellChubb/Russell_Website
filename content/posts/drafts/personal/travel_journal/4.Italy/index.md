## 4th - 🇮🇹

After taking a tram back to the airport (*and remembering to validate our card on the way out*), we boarded our Flixbus, and made our way to Milan over the course of 5 hours.

## 6th - 🇮🇹

Our next travel day! I awoke with a sore stomach, packed our stuff from our moist accomodation, and walked to the train station to catch our train out to Bellano (*on Lake Como*).

As the train travelled further and further out of Milan, I remember seeing more and more people looking out the window as the scenary as the view got better and better. Eventually, we hit our stop and pulled out directions to our accomodation, which Olivia told me was "*very close to the train station*".

Now, one thing about Bellano, the roads are very small, and there isn't really any sort of footpath. So, Olivia and I ended up starting our walk to the accomodation, before turning back and accessing our options. After a small spat between us, we decided to just walk on the road and try our best to dodge cars. Now, here's the really fun part about when Olivia said the acomodation was "*very close to the train station*", well she wasn't wrong, as the crow flies, it was very close, the only issue was that it was straight up a hill. So after lugging Olivia's pack up a massive hill for 25 minutes, we finally made it to our accomodation.

For the rest of the day, after resting up and unpacking, we decided to head to the supermarket to stock up, before lazing about in the afternoon sun, and taking in the wonderful views.

The day concluded with a "*home-cooked*" meal from yours truly, a delightful little red sauce with some raviolli.
