## 1st - 🇫🇷

Olivia and I woke early, said a final goodbye to my parents and then set off across Barcelona to the bus-station. Something for the rest of these articles, Olivia was travelling with a bigger bag than me, and because I'm bigger, whenever we moved about, I was tasked with taking her bag. In Barcelona, this was the first time that I had to actually move this bag around, which was a 30 minute walk across Barcelona, which doesn't sound to bad, but Olivia's bag was so uncomfortable on my back, that it made 30 minutes feel like 3 hours.

Regardless, after searching around the bus-depot, we finally found our guy, and strapped in for our 10 hour bus-ride to Nice. During the trip, we decided to book in a few pieces of accomodation / transport, to make use of the "*dead-time*". I'll note that the bus we were on had wifi, and so I was able to do some work on one of my projects (*on the computer*), which then in turn allowed me to complete my first (*of many*) Git Commits from a moving vehicle.

After getting off the bus, we managed to get on the tram to central Nice, however, we made the mistake of not validating our ticket before getting off the train, which caused us to get stuck in the train station with no options to get out. Thankfully, a local took pity on us, and let us trail him out.

Once we got to central Nice, we walked to our first accomodation, which turned out to be a bit of a horror show. The actual accomodation was fine, however, when we first booked, we were promised some kind of air-conditioning (*which is critial in European Summers*), however, we found none! Now I know this kind of makes Olivia and I sound like pussies, but we couldn't open the windows at night because we were right next to a busy trainline, thus, the choice was either boil, or not sleep. After messaging the host and quering this, they finally dropped off a small desk-fan, which was quite humorous looking back.

## 2nd - 🇫🇷

The next day, we went out for a 10KM run to explore the city by foot, and ending up putting up a solid stop-and-start effort where we ventured up, down, and along the main beach promenade.

After that, we relaxed for a bit and started watching Geordie Shore, and went on a bit of a marathon. For dinner, we had some fun and went out to a Korean BBQ Place to eat (*kind of a weird choice in Nice, but still*). I love Korean BBQ, but this particular outing was quite expensive, but in my humble opinion, still so worth it.
