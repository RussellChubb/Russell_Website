## 9th - 🇱🇺

On the day of the 9th, we awoke, packed our bags and began heading to the train station in Frankfurt, before realising that trains "*sleep-in*" on Sundays, and thus we had to get a tram to the bus-stop where we were catching our bus to Luxembourg.

After a short flix-bus, we arrived at the Luxembourg Bus Terminal (*It's a carpark*), where we then put in directions, and headed to our accomodation.

Funny story about the accomodation, the AirBNB we booked basically ended up being a room in some dudes flat (*Danish was his name*). At first I didn't think he had a job, because it was a Monday when I asked him "*what he was doing today?*", and he basically just replied “*might just chill around the house*”. For reference, I later found out that he an accountant / finance analyst of some type, but was on Holiday and was§ chilling at his crib before taking off over-seas.

I haven't talked much about the accomodation Olivia and I have been staying in, but this one was pretty large, certain areas are were quite messy, but it’s kind of funny anyway. God bless you Danish.

We finished the day by heading to the supermarket to pick up some food, before settling down in our room to watch the Oddysey on my computer from a video that someone had recorded on their cell-phone in a Spanish movie-theater (*just the way Nolan intended*)

## 10th - 🇱🇺

On the 10th, Olivia and I woke feeling refreshed, had a quick bite, and then booked in the last of our buses, accomodation and activities for the trip.

After we completed our admin, we went sight-seeing, taking a look at:

* Adolphe Bridge (*Walked across a few bridges, but this one has the best view / is the most famous*)
* Casemates du Bock (*We didn’t go in, as it cost, but had a good look around outside*)
* Palais Grand-Ducal
* Place de la Constitution (*Nice little garden here*ß)
* Cathedral Notre Dame (*They have one here lol*)

We also purchased our Souvenirs (*and me some new sun-glasses, rip to tha GOATß), while at the same time questioning the health and safety of this kids ride that spun them around near the edge of a 50M cliff.

After all that sight-seeing, Olivia and I were absolutely famished, and so we sat down for a few cold beers at a pub. Apparently the beer didn't quite quence Olivia's thirst, as we continued into a Five-Guys for a milkshake (*which even I'll admit, tasted amazing*)

After that, we picked up some salads from the supermarket, and then mished home before finishing the night with the new (*very average*), spiderman movie.

## 11th - 🇱🇺

On the 11th, Olivia and I both slept in quite late, before asking Danish (*our AirBNB host*) if he had any reccomendations for gyms nearby, and Danish said yes, and told us that we can his gym-pass! - ergo we went to "*Basic Fit*" and had a good workout.

Afterwards, we got Matcha Tea's, and I don't have any more notes for this day...
