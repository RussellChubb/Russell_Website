## Introduction

I collected some notes over our trip, with the intention of creating a bit of a story at the end of it! - As such, I've begun at the very beginning of the trip, when we first landed down in London!

### 22/05

Our journey begins on the 22nd of May, 2026, as Olivia and I arrive in Heathrow Airport in London. We arrivied in fairly good spirits, although I was beginning to tire of cramped airplanes and movies.

As we made our way out of the Airport, one the things I remember being most suprised about was how easily it was to clear customs, as Olivia and I were both prepared for it to be a bit of a slog (*similar to how it is in New Zealand*). The other thing that I was looking forward to was the warm weather, which to be fair, I wasn't exactly expecting flying into London, but lowe and behold, it was about 25 degree's and clear blue skies!

The next step was getting from the airport to Rians, which, after asking around for directions to the tube, and managed to bumble our way onto a line into the central city, before then catching the caledonian line (*which I'll add, any London line on peak-hours is hell to try and get on with luggage*) out to my brothers place.

<!-- TODO -->
After introductions were exchanged with Olivia and Maria (*as well as many hugs!*), we decided to go out to get a bit of dinner, and setteled on a pub (*google the name*), as well as a pint! - which was super dope, given it was my first experience with English pubs at the time, which I actually learned that people sit at the bar and they talk to each other, a concept which, to be honest, I couldn't wrap my Oceanic brain around, however, I did appreciate the concept.

Now, for anyone wondering about Jet-lag, although we were tired, Olivia was actually charging on quite well, attributing her stamina to the many night-shift sequences she endured. However, after settling the bill and walking back, to my brothers place (*where we were crashing on his couch*), we hit the hay at 9:00PM and called it a day!

### 23/05

Our first real day of travel started the way any strong day should start... A bunch of greasy food from a local "*dirty spoon*".

My brother Rian took particular offense to some of the orders that Olivia and I made, specifically that it's "*weird to put cheese on baked beans on toast*" and when I ordered a vegetarian meal, he noted that "*people will you look at you weird for ordering that here*".

### 24/05

What a fun day this was! Looking back on this day from the future, it one of my favourites. The big highlight's that I've written about this day were Olivia and I going to our very first Premier League Football Game! The game in question is that Leeds vs West Ham Match.

This is such a bitter-sweet game, and created the weirdest vibe I've ever seen at a stadium. For reference, West Ham beat leads 3-1 (*check this*), but because of a game between two other teams (*check which ones*), they were still relegated from a points perspective. Something interesting that I didn't know about before coming to England, is that in Premier League Games, you're not allowed to bring drinks into stands, which means that you need to watch from this little outside bar area before you can come back in. This then leads to an interesting behaviour I noticed wherein dudes will go for a drink, because they don't want to miss anything, will then slam their drink and then go back to their seat. Bro, why not just let people drink from their seat?

Afterwards, we walked out and along the London Canals wherein we stopped in at this Venezuelean food, which was so incredibly good, (*up there as one of the best things I've ever eaten*).

Now, I'll write this here, because it'll show you the insertion point for a story that rounds out 3 months later, but as we walked into this offie (*off-license, it's sort of like a dairy for my New Zealand folks*) we smelled someone incredible, the scent was a mix of vanilla and mango. Anyway, long story short, we ask him the name of the colonge, and he just says "*Mango*"... cmon bro, what do you mean just "*Mango*". Anyway, this interaction started a small research-project of mine to try and work out what the name of the cologne he was wearing.

Something else about this game was that Arsenal won the Premier League, so the streets were lined with people in red shirts, in differing states of inebriation shouting, laughing, honking horns and having a jolly time.

(*this jumps forward very far into the future, to the end of our trip*)

## 25th - 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁥󠁮󠁧󠁿

Leaving Europe, sad, but optimistic vibes were floating about the air. Olivia and I woke earlier than normal (*normal being about 10AM haha*) and went to the same place as yesterday for Breakfast (*it was so good we had to run it back*). We then proceeded to back our bags and check out of the hostel in preperation for our 9+ hour bus ride.

We ran into some trouble in the border, it seems liked the technology on the French side just wasn’t working, the guy that was handling Olivia was so intrigued by the black NZ Passport. With me, they questioned me a little bit about what day I got into Europe, and (*I think*) trying to work out whether or not I violated my Schengen days.

This confused me sligtly as we were leaving France, rather than trying to get in, but eventually it felt like the guy gave up and let me through. I will note though, even though they were questioning us, they were still quite nice about the whole affair.

The English side of the border was much more optimized, Olivia and I both got a really nice guy who we had some banter with! Once we cleared both sides of the border, it was time for our bus to be loaded into these carriages and taking taken through the under-water tunnel to the UK (*can’t really say much about this, I was too intrigued with Jonny Depp in Pirates of the Caribbean  2*)

Once we arrived in London, we could immediately feel the slightly more, tense, rushed vibe in the air. Probably about 5 minutes after we got off our first train, we walked past a guy who had been hit in the head by a woman he was supposedly creeping on Anyway, they sent about 6 police cars, and even though there was a bit of blood, 6 cars is probably overkill.

Once we arrived at Rian's, we had a good chat and a pizza that Maria bought us (*Thanks Maria*), afterwards we ended the day at Rian's local pub (*The Prince Albert*) and polished off a few guinesses's.

## 26th - 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁥󠁮󠁧󠁿

I don't have many notes on this day, but I assume we probably just walked around, and took it easy after a few months of on the go travel.

We finished the evening in a similar fashion to yesterday... At the Prince Albert with a guiness. It's funny the peeople you meet and the influence they have on you. For instance, I met one of the local's at Rian's pub called Phil. Now, Phil is a very stero-typical English Bloke, you know the type. Clean shave head, bit red, bit of a belly, loud and out-spoken, but all in all a good bloke. I can't remember what we were talking about, other than I was explaining how Meridian had an idea to use lasers to deter pigeons from gate structures (*he was really entertained by this*), so entertained infact that he offered me a line, which I declined (*Mostly because it was a wednesday*).

The other bloke that we were drinking with was a bloke called John. John's an older fella with a heart of gold. Made his living as the manager of one of the gardens in central London. Anyway, John went to the pub every night, and on this particular night, poor john was under the weather, which I assume lead to him getting slightly more rinsed than usual, I think I caught him almost falling a couple of times.

We ended the night by walking John back to his home, just up the road.

## 27th - 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁥󠁮󠁧󠁿

I started this day by applying to some more jobs while Olivia rested, and once she rose (*like a Phoenix*), we ventured into town to take a look around / do some shopping.

We fuelled Olivia up with a blue matcha, which I'll note is one of the better matcha's from all of the matcha's that we consumed on this trip.

My note that I'm writing this from just says that "*Oxford Circus is a mess*", what I can remember from Oxford circus is that we came during the peak foot-traffic period, and anyway, it was so crowded and loud, and generally overstimulating.

Now, this is the point wherein we round out the "Mango" cologne saga. We eventually find a store that has Mango Ouid Gritti, wherin I go and try it on, and while it's super nice, it still doesn't justify it's price tag. As such, we end up walking out of the store empty handed.

## 28th - 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁥󠁮󠁧󠁿

I started the day applying for more jobs, which I wouldn't actually reccomend anyone do, as most of my best job prospects just came from having a well configured LinkedIn.

Rian and I then met up with Cyrus and his son Irwin at a Cafe called Rua, which Rian remarked was "*the kind of place where people are shooting up, but also affluent white people all go*". We then took Irwin to the petting zoo, which was super cool to see in the middle of a city.

I then had a bit of a quiet middle part of the day as I was quite tired. Later on Rian and I watched the darts and womens volleyball. Later on, Olivia and I met up with Holly at a bar called "Falcon". The vibe was good, the beers were flowing and we enjoyed playing cards.

After leaving the bar (*with our drinks in hand might I add*), we then decided we needed a scran (*a feed*). Olivia mentioned that she’s never had Nandos before, so we grabbed her a quick one. She hated it lowkey because they mixed my spicy chicken in with her mild one, ergo she couldn’t eat it lol (*more food for me haha*)

## 29th - 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁥󠁮󠁧󠁿

The final full day of travel, very mixed feelings here. For one, I was starting to get very tired of sleeping in small cramped spaces, but for two, it meant the start of something new.

I started the day with some eggs, kimchi, cheese, pickles and chilli oil! Olivia and I then went to a gym to get a workout in, which we were very much craving after months of very minimal exerition. Afterwards, we picked up a cheeky meal deal and walked to Highbury Park. On our way to the park, a lady in a rough way asked us to top up her Oyster card so she could get home, poor girl looked a terrible state. So Olivia topped her card up, which I've noted because it highlights Olivia's generosity, as I likely wouldn't have done the same.

At Highbury park, we ate our food and remarked on how the trees were starting to lose their leaves, and how the breeze was starting to get a bit colder. We also walked around the fair that was on at the time!

We then walked back to Maria and Rians, and rounded out the greatest adventure of my life.

If you've just read this entry, feel free to read the rest. However, if you've read everything, thanks for sharing in some of the fondest memories I've ever had, with some of the coolest people I've ever met, in the greatest summer of my life.
