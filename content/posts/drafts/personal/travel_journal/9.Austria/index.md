## 29th - 🇦🇹

On the 29th, we checked out of Maverick Hostel and then caught a bus to Vienna, where after a short train ride, we checked in Wombats Hostel in our 8 bedroom dorm.

It was during this bus-ride that I reflected on how cross-cultural memes are. For example, while in Greece, Olvia and I were walking past a group of kids, probably aged around 8-10, and I pulled out the "6-7" hand gesture at them, wherein they proceeded to loose their minds. Also in Greece, a man was on his phone in the the street, scrolling reels, wherein he played one that went “*fahhhhhhh*” and I repeated it back and we cracked up.

When we first arrvied off the bus, I first noticed the heat, which although Austria isn't particularly hot normally, it was experiencing somewhat of a heat-wave around the time we where there, as such, I remember it was quite a sweaty walk with our bags to the hostel.

It was this particular hostel that was one of the hotter ones of the trip, and in a 8 man room with very little air conditioning, it did end up being quite warm at night.

## 30th - 🇦🇹

On the 30th, we met some of our other dorm-mates, Mixzu, Brady, Will, Nieue, of which, Will and Brady were both Australian, doing a very similar OE to Olivia and I, (*except with far more partying*).

I discussed the heat in yesterdays insert, but today was meant to be peak temperatures, as such, to beat the heat, Olivia and I decided to go to the Danube and hit one of the local swimming spots, wherein we basically just spent the entire day either swimming, or lazing in the sun. One fun story from this was that at one point, Olivia went looking for the toilets, and in her search, ended up accidentally walking into the nudist bathing section.

After we arrived back, we went to the Hostel Bar for a few beers with our roommates, wherein Mixzu (*big Japenese dude*) got so drunk that he smashed not one, but two glasses of beer. Will and Brady also made a convincing case for us to come to a festival they were going to tomorrow, and so we decided to take them up on their offer.

## 31st - 🇦🇹

Since we’d spent the whole day at the river yesterday and were leaving tomorrow, we decided to cram as much sightseeing into one day as we could.

We visited St Charles Church (Karlskirche), admired the Vienna State Opera building, and stopped by the Albertina. We also wandered through Sigmund Freud Park and past the Votivkirche, where Olivia said the halo-like structure at the top looked like a roller coaster.

From there, we visited the Sigmund Freud House and Vienna University, then saw the city hall, the film festival, and Vienna Park. We rounded out the day with Volksgarten and Parliament.

During the day I also spent some time reminiscing about some of our terrible inside jokes this trip (*feel free to ask us what any of them mean*)

* Copper
* Downtown Abbey
* Largest Equine Structure in the World

Then came the festival, after we finished site-seeing, we went back to our Hostel and got ready to go to **Paradise Gardens**. After linking it back up with our roommates, we all left to take our 3 train journey to the venue.

One thing about one of my notes from this section is that I say this "*I’ll make a note that in Vienna, and quite a few other places, you can be kind of cheeky with not purchasing train tickets, as there is no validation system in place*, this is quite funny how fast this note came to bite us in the butt.

So, after being a bit cheeky and not purchasing tickets, we began our journey out to the venue, which was quite a warm experience, as the trains had no AC, so I was at the full-force of the summer heat.

After walking about 10 minutes from the train-station, we made it to the entrance of the festival, which was a super dope location, sort of like in a forest with a castle on it. Also with a recently constructed an octagonal stage from wood which was cool!

The actual music they were playing at the festival was dope as well, kind of like a mix of house, techno, garage etc. One thing I’ll comment on regarding differences between New Zealand festivals and European ones is that in New Zealand, we’re far more feral, with mosh’s that can become quite argy-bargy. This is contrasted to Europe where the crowd was very relaxed, dressed really well and in general I’d say was 10-20x cooler.

Also the drinks line took like less than 3 minutes to get a beverage at any point in the evening, very contrasted to how lackadaisical and disorganised we are in New Zealand.

They also had some cool activities to do for example, they had:

* Make your own tie-dye bandana
* Draw yourself
* Those tattoos you apply with water and pressure.

Anyway, the gig finished at 1, and because we had to sort of carry this one Australian chick back, we had to run to catch the last train of the night, or risk facing a 100€ taxi!
