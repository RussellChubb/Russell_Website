* The Festival…
    * After we finished site-seeing, we went back to our Hostel and got ready to go to the festival (Paradise Garden)
    * Managed to sneak in a cheeky LinkedIn post while I was here…
    * Our roommates arrived back and then we all left to take our 3 train journey to the venue. I’ll make a note that in Vienna (and quite a few other places, you can be kind of cheeky with not purchasing train tickets, as there is no validation system in place)
    * Trains had no AC so we were boiling in the summer heat!
    * Got to the venue, super dope location, sort of like in a forest with a castle on it
    * They’d also recently constructed an octagonal stage from wood which was cool!
    * The music was really cool, kind of like a mix of house, techno, garage etc
    * One thing I’ll comment on regarding differences between New Zealand festivals and European ones is that in New Zealand, we’re far more feral, with mosh’s that can become quite argy-bargy. This is contrasted to Europe where the crowd was very relaxed, dressed really well and in general I’d say was 10-20x cooler.
    * Also the drinks line took like less than 3 minutes to get a beverage at any point in the evening, very contrasted to how lackadaisical and disorganised we are in New Zealand.
    * They also had some cool activities to do for example, they had:
        * Make your own tie-dye bandana
        * Draw yourself
        * Those tattoos you apply with water and pressure.
    * Anyway, the gig finished at 1, and because we had to sort of carry this one Australian chick back, we had to run to catch the last train of the night, or risk facing a 100€ taxi!
