## Notes for this Blog post

[Saw a discussion on Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/USNEWS/comments/1voca1r/luigi_mangione_admits_killing_brian_thompson_says/) on how they caught Luigi Mangione, I find it increasingly unlikely that he was spotted by a worker.

The McDonald's employee phone call was likely just a case of "*parallel construction*".

The police used some (*most likely illegal*) form of surveillance to find Luigi. They then knew they couldn't just wander in because they would have had to say how they found him which would get thrown out in court.

So a random plain clothes cop walked up to the McDonald's employee and said "*hey that guy over there looks like that guy who killed a ceo recently. You should call the police to report them*". Then, Police hiding conveniently around the corner swarm in immediately.

For now, this is an America problem, but, New Zealand is just little America in a lot of ways, thus, it won't be long before Flock (*or another solution*), trys to take hold in New-Zealand.

I can constrast this with another example that New Zealand has been late to front to, that of Data-Centers, protests have only been organized just now, however, we saw the writing on the wall for a long time.

If the people want any chance of being able to keep this illegal, unethical and immoral surveilance technology out of New Zealand, we need to express our displeasure earlier and more vocally.
