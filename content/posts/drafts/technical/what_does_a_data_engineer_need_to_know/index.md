## Notes for this Blog post

* One of the first steps I took as a Data Analyst was to identify what a Data-Analyst actually needs to know.

* I'm making this as I've been aspirational about making a move into more technical work, and the way to do this is by choosing either Data-Engineering, or Science.

* Predicting a move to a jack-of-all-trades analyst that can work end-to-end on data-projects. i.e. source data, create pipelines (*traditional DE stuff*), perform advanced analysis (*all the way into ML stuff*), and then visualise and present findings (*traditional data analyst stuff*)

* ^ As such, I'm preparing for this to happen, and up-skilling into DE, and then trying my hand later on at some DS work.
