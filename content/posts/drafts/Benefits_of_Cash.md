## Notes for this Blog post

Fundamentally I don't like how Telemetry has very casually eased it's way into almost every single application as well as in our hardware.

I understand how useful this data is for companies to use (*I actively use certain telemetry data for my work*)

At the same time, I have a belief that most people don't understand what exactly a few things actually mean, and what companies (*or motivated individuals*), can actually do.

I'd like touch on the following:

* Telemetry
* Cookies
* How users aren't compensated for this data, even with products they pay for (*If it's free, you are the product*)

**NOTE:** I'd like to tie in the the whole "*don't make me download an app thing from a previous post into this one*".
