## Overview 📌

Welcome to my Blog! - I've created this space as a way to capture and communicate ideas, thoughts, musing's and anything else that comes out of my brain.

### Why host a Blog? ❓

I've always envied people who can write well. People who can capture the essence of life with prose that hooks readers into the story they weave.

As such, this space is a great way for me to practice my writing, and general communication, through a medium that I find super fun to utilize (*The Web!*)

I've also always wanted a space for me to be able to put the "*scripts*", that I create for my YouTube Channel somewhere, such that if someone doesn't want to watch a video, they can instead read a blog post!
