## Preface

Hello all!

You may be aware that Olivia and I engaged in a bit of an OE (*Overseas Experience*) during the summer of 2026.

Each day during this trip, I collected notes about the contents of the day, interesting sites, things that happened etc.

I did this with the intention of creating a digital scrapbook, such that I could look back at all the memories (*good or bad*) that we enjoyed during the trip.

Our journey begins on the 22nd of May, 2026, as Olivia and I arrived at Heathrow Airport in London. We arrived in fairly good spirits, although I was beginning to tire of cramped aeroplanes and movies.

As we made our way out of the airport, one of the things I was most surprised by was how easy it was to clear customs. Olivia and I were both prepared for it to be a bit of a slog, (*similar to how it is in New Zealand*) however, we had no such trouble.

The next step was getting from the airport to my brother Rian's place. He was kind enough to open up his heart and home to Olivia and me on a couple of occasions while we were travelling.

After asking around for directions to the Tube, we managed to bumble our way onto a line into the city centre, then caught the Piccadilly line (*which, I'll add, is hell to get on with luggage during peak hours*) out to my brother's place in Islington.

After hugs and introductions were exchanged with Olivia and Maria (*Rian's fiancée at the time, and now wife*), we decided to go out for dinner and a pint. We settled on a pub called [the Lord Stanley](https://www.thelordstanley.com/), which Rian hailed as a great example of a "*traditional English pub*."

After walking into the Lord Stanley, I quickly learned that in British pubs, people sit at the bar and talk to each other (*as you can see by the three blokes in red*). To be honest, I couldn't wrap my antipodean brain around the concept, but I did appreciate it none the less.

Now, for anyone wondering about jet lag, although we were tired, Olivia was actually charging on quite well, attributing her stamina to the many night-shift sequences she endured during her time at Masterton Medical Centre.

However, after settling the bill and walking back to Rian's, we hit the hay at 9:00 p.m. and called it a day!

## 25/09

Olivia and I woke earlier than usual (*usually about 10 a.m., haha*) and went to the same cafe as the day before for breakfast (*it was so good we had to run it back*).

We then packed our bags and checked out The People Hostel (*Paris Belleville*) in preparation for our nine-hour-plus flix-bus ride.

Everything was running smoothly, until we had to exit the bus to complete border control, wherein we ran into some trouble (*merde*).

<!-- Border Image -->
{{< figure src="https://static.independent.co.uk/s3fs-public/thumbnails/image/2019/03/06/10/falls-road-ireland-060319.jpg" alt="The actual border featured less barbed wire and guns" caption="The actual French border featured less barbed wire and guns" figureClass="grid-w100" >}}

The French Border Control Agents asked me what day I had arrived in Europe, as well as my movements. I think they were trying to work out whether I had overstayed my Schengen allowance, but I suppose we'll never know.

Olivia had less trouble, as it seemed like her agent was more interested in showing the other staff Olivia's black passport. The whole experience slightly confused me, as it's not like we were trying to enter France, quite the opposite.

Eventually, it felt like the guy gave up and let me through. I'll note though, even though they were questioning us, they were still quite friendly about the whole affair.

After we crossed the French Border, we were loaded back onto the bus for 5 minutes, before we had to unload once again to clear the English side of the border, however, this time the English were much more optimized. Olivia and I both got a really nice border control agent, and we had some banter with him!

Once we cleared both sides of the border, our bus was loaded into one of these carriages and taken through the underwater tunnel to the UK (*can't really say much about this; I was too intrigued by Johnny Depp in Pirates of the Caribbean 2*).

<!-- Image of the Channel -->
{{< figure src="https://www.bechtel.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/161579-1-1024x1016.webp" alt="Diagram of the Channel Tunnel" caption="Interesting, huh?" figureClass="grid-w100" >}}

Once we arrived in London, we could immediately feel the slightly more tense, rushed vibe in the air. About five minutes after we got off our first train, we walked past a guy who had been hit in the head by a woman he was supposedly creeping on.

The police sent about six cars. Even though there was a bit of blood, six cars was probably overkill.

Once we arrived at Rian's, we had a good chat and a pizza that Maria bought us (*thanks, Maria!*). Afterwards, we ended the day at Rian's local pub, [the Prince Edward](https://londonpubsgroup.camra.org.uk/viewnode.php?id=48851), and polished off a few pints of Guinness.

## 26/09

I don't have many notes on this day, but I assume we probably just walked around and took it easy after a few months of being on the go.

We finished the evening in a similar fashion to the day before... at the Prince Edward with a Guinness.

It's funny how the people you meet can influence you. For instance, I met one of the locals at Rian's pub, a bloke called Phil. Now, Phil is a very stereotypical English bloke, you know the type: clean-shaven, a bit red, a bit of a belly, loud and outspoken, but all in all, a good bloke.

I can't remember what we were talking about, but I was explaining how Meridian had an idea to use lasers to deter pigeons from gate structures (*he had a similar pigeon problem at his gaff*).

Funny the things you remember huh?

{{< figure src="https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/71zMdWsSHiL._AC_UF894,1000_QL80_.jpg" alt="" caption="Pigeon Time hehe" figureClass="grid-w100" >}}

The other bloke we were drinking with was called John. John's an older fella with a heart of gold who ventures to the Prince Edward most nights.

On this particular night, poor John was under the weather, which I assume led to him getting slightly more rinsed than usual, I think I caught him almost falling over a couple of times.

We ended the night by walking John back to his home, just up the road.

## 29/09

I started the day applying for more jobs, which I wouldn't actually recommend anyone do, as most of my best job prospects came from having a well-configured LinkedIn profile.

Rian and I then met up with Cyrus and his son Irwin at a cafe called [Rua](https://www.instagram.com/ruacoffeebar/?hl=en), which Rian remarked was "*the kind of place where people are shooting up, but also where affluent white people go*." We then took Irwin to the petting zoo, which was super cool to see in the middle of a city.

{{< figure src="https://file.garden/atcrboPYCQOy1FQn/England/Petting_Zoo" alt="" caption="The Geese looks dangerous here" figureClass="grid-w100" >}}

I then had a quiet middle part of the day, as I was quite tired. Later, Rian and I watched the darts and women's volleyball.

As darkness fell, Olivia and I met up with Holly at a bar called [the Falcon](https://www.thefalconclapham.co.uk/#/). The vibe was good, the beers were flowing, and we enjoyed playing cards.

After leaving the bar (*with our drinks in hand, might I add*), we decided we needed a scran (*a feed*). Olivia mentioned that she'd never had Nando's before, so we grabbed her one.

She hated it, low-key, because they mixed my spicy chicken in with her mild one. Ergo, she couldn't eat it, lol (*more food for me, haha*).

## 30/09

It was the final full day of travel, and I had very mixed feelings. On the one hand, I was starting to get very tired of sleeping in a different bed every couple of nights; on the other, it meant the start of something new.

I started the day with some eggs, kimchi, cheese, pickles, and chilli oil! Olivia and I then went to a gym to get a workout in, which we were very much craving after months of very minimal exercise.

Afterwards, we picked up a cheeky meal deal and walked to Highbury Park. On the way, a woman who seemed to be having a rough time asked us to top up her Oyster card so she could get home. The poor girl looked in a terrible state, so Olivia topped up her card.

{{< figure src="https://i.redd.it/vym99doeshsz.png" alt="" caption="I like this meme, no clue what it means at all." figureClass="grid-w100" >}}

I mention this because it highlights Olivia's generosity (*as I likely wouldn't have done the same*).

At Highbury Park, we ate our food and remarked on how the trees were starting to lose their leaves and the breeze was getting a bit colder. We also walked around the fair that was on at the time!

We then walked back to Maria and Rian's. That officially marked the end of our OE and wrapped up the summer of a lifetime.
